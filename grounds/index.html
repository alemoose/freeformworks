Good questions

Let’s clear
a few things up.

Can I subscribe today?

Not yet. Grounds is an early MVP. Contact Freeform Works to discuss access. No checkout, billing, or paid subscription is currently available.

Does Grounds measure my customer’s lawn?

No. You enter an estimate of the mowable lawn area. Pricing uses your configured rates. We do not scrape property listings or claim satellite or AI measurements.

Will it send messages to my customers?

No. Follow-ups are drafts. You approve quotes, share them manually, and record the customer’s decision.

Can I use it from my truck?

The workspace is designed for phones and larger screens. You need an internet connection; offline mode is not available.

How is customer information protected?

The app requires owner authentication and checks business ownership on the server. Public forms never expose other customers’ records.
