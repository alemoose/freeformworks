Privacy policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Freeform Works is Agustin Lemuz-Juarez and María Estrada, a two-person web studio in Tuscaloosa, Alabama. This page explains what we collect on freeformworks.com and what we do with it. Questions go to hello@freeformworks.com.

What we collect

We only collect what you type into our booking form at freeformworks.com/book:

  • your name and email address
  • your business name and type of business
  • your phone number, if you choose to give it
  • the day and time you picked for the call

How we use it

We use it to set up your call: to put it on our calendar, send you the invite and video link, and get in touch about the call. If you leave a phone number, we use it to call you at that time.

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with anyone for advertising.

Where it's kept

When you book, our site creates an event on our own Google Calendar with the details above, and Google emails the invite to you and to us. Google handles that under its own privacy policy.

The site runs on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel keeps basic server logs, such as IP addresses and browser type, to run and protect the site.

Our Google Calendar connection

The booking page connects to our own Google Calendar, never yours. It reads when we're busy so it only shows open times, and it adds your call to our calendar. It doesn't see or change anything in your Google account.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't set cookies and doesn't use analytics, ad trackers or outside scripts.

How long we keep it

Booking details stay on our calendar as a record of the call. Email us and we'll show you what we have, correct it, or delete it.

Changes

If this changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

The rules for using the site are in our terms of service.
