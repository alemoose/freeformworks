Working together · 04

What you get when you hire Freeform Works.

Not a deck, not a sales pipeline, not a junior account manager. A single designer-builder, working in your season, with a portfolio you'll actually send to your peers.

01

One person on the file

No account-manager handoff. The person designing the site is the person writing the code and the person picking up the phone at 4 pm.

02

Real custom code

Hand-written HTML, CSS, and a thin layer of JavaScript. No Squarespace, no Wix, no drag-and-drop dependency you can't escape.

03

Performance you can sell

Sub-half-second loads and 95+ Lighthouse by default. Sites that feel faster than the ones your clients are leaving.

04

An AI-accelerated pace

Two to three weeks to ship, not three months. The boring parts get automated; the taste stays human.

05

Maintenance, quietly

Optional monthly care. Copy edits, new galleries, small features. Priced per scope. Cancel anytime, the site is yours.

06

A portfolio you'll send

Editorial register, not template-shaped. The kind of work you'd actually link from your bio without the small apology.
