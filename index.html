What I build · 02

Six common builds. Each one is a starting point, not a fixed package.

Restaurants

For Restaurants

  • Direct ordering through DoorDash, Uber Eats, or Grubhub at 0% commission, replacing the 15-30% they take on app orders.
  • A menu you own instead of scattered across places.singleplatform.com, Linktree, and the apps.
  • Reservation system built-in or via OpenTable embed.
  • Hours, location, and contact kept consistent across Google, Facebook, and the site.
  • Press and reviews consolidated from Yelp, Tripadvisor, Google, and any feature mentions.
  • Fast on mobile because that's where most orders start.

Principles · 03

Five things Freeform Works keeps coming back to. A working draft, not a manifesto.

[ 01 ]

The work is the marketing. Everything else is overhead.

[ 02 ]

A page that loads in half a second wins arguments a beautiful one can't.

[ 03 ]

Templates are how studios end up looking like their competitors. Refuse them.

[ 04 ]

Maintenance is a craft, not a fee. Quiet edits beat loud redesigns.

[ 05 ]

AI accelerates the boring parts. The taste is still the work.
