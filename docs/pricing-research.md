## 2. What owners already pay for software

This matters because booking and the owner dashboard compete with these, not with web studios.

**Shops (barbers, salons, photographers):**
- Square Appointments: free for one location; $49 or $149/mo for paid tiers.
- GlossGenius: $24 to $148/mo.
- Vagaro: about $30/mo, plus about $10 per extra calendar.

**Trades (lawn care, landscaping, contractors):**
- Jobber: about $39 to $249/mo, up to $599 for big teams.
- Housecall Pro: about $59 to $329/mo.
- Lawn and landscape websites: $1,800 to $8,500 typical (projectcostestimator.com).
- Marketing retainers for landscapers: $950/mo at the low end, $3,000 to $5,000/mo typical per the National Association of Landscape Professionals blog.

## 3. What this means for us

1. **The current "$1,500 to $2,500" range is a problem on its own.** A range makes every owner ask "which end am I?" and pushes them back to "contact us," which is the thing we are trying to beat. Two fixed prices are clearer.
2. **Shops have cheap options already.** A barber can get booking free from Square and a $49/mo site. For shops we have to win on a custom site, booking on their own domain, and two people they can text. The price has to sit near the low end of the custom range.
3. **Trades are where the money is.** They already pay $39 to $329/mo for Jobber-type software and get quoted $950+/mo for marketing. A crew owner who loses one $300 job a month to a missed text is losing more than our monthly fee. There's room to charge more here, as long as the dashboard is real and supported.
4. **We are a two-person student studio.** Pricing at agency level ($4,000+) would invite comparisons we lose on track record. Pricing below $800 puts us with template shops and attracts clients who want unlimited work for nothing. The middle is right.

## 4. Recommended prices (for Agustin and María to decide)

| | Shops | Trades and crews |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who | Barbers, salons, photographers, florists, restaurants | Lawn care, landscaping, contractors |
| Setup | **$1,200** one time | **$2,500** one time |
| Includes | Custom website, booking on your own domain (ours, or your existing Square/GlossGenius/Vagaro), Google profile cleanup | Custom website, quote requests, scheduling, owner dashboard for jobs and customers |
| Monthly | **$75/mo**: hosting, edits, updates | **$200/mo**: hosting, edits, updates, dashboard support |
| First year total | $2,100 | $4,900 |

Why these numbers:
- **$1,200 shop setup** is above template shops ($499 to $1,299) and below most custom studios ($1,800 to $2,000), for a fully custom site.
- **$75/mo** sits between hosting-only ($49) and hosting plus edits ($99).
- **$2,500 trades setup** is at the low end of what landscaping sites cost ($1,800 to $8,500) and includes a dashboard those sites don't have.
- **$200/mo** is between Jobber's middle plans ($129 and $249) and far below any marketing retainer ($950+).
- **Both first-year totals** are under half of The Nine's $10,000 minimum.

Things to decide before publishing:
- **Commitment.** Month-to-month or a minimum term? Many local competitors lock in 6 to 18 months, so "cancel anytime" would set us apart.
- **Dashboard workload.** The trades tier means supporting software that crews use every day. $200/mo has to cover the time that takes.
- **Ownership.** Who owns the site and the domain if a client leaves? Several competitors keep it. Saying "you own it" is a real advantage if it's true.
